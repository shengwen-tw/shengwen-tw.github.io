## Overview

From May 18 to 20, I traveled to Minneapolis for Open Source Summit North America 2026, a Linux Foundation event, together with [Yung-Tse Cheng (Mes)](https://mes0903.github.io/), [Chi-sheng Chen](https://github.com/rota1001), and [Jim Huang (jserv)](https://github.com/jserv). With Mes, we presented our work on VirtIO-GPU support in [semu](https://github.com/sysprog21/semu), a lightweight system emulator capable of running Linux.

My involvement with semu began in May 2023 after hearing jserv introduce the project at [CTHPC](https://sites.google.com/view/cthpc2023/program). Since then, I have worked mostly on VirtIO-related features, including virtio-blk, virtio-rng, virtio-gpu, and virtio-input. Graphics virtualization eventually became the main focus of my contribution, and Mes later pushed the work further with better 2D support, virtio-input improvements, virtio-gpu 3D, and virglrenderer integration.

Beyond the conference, we also visited the University of Minnesota, the Minneapolis Institute of Art, the Minneapolis Sculpture Garden, and Minnehaha Falls.

## Technical Highlights

On the second day, Chi-sheng and jserv presented ["Optimize Linux Kernel To Fit Microcontrollers With 1 MB RAM"](https://osselcna2026.sched.com/event/2JQvE/optimize-linux-kernel-to-fit-microcontrollers-with-1-mb-ram-jim-huang-chisheng-chen-national-cheng-kung-university?iframe=no), showing how a No-MMU Linux kernel can run on STM32 F4 and H7 platforms through XIP and various size-reduction techniques.

![703752275_10164439127852389_4963253938985319027_n](https://hackmd.io/_uploads/Skn09qglfx.jpg)

On the third day, jserv also gave ["Construct a Lean and Fast RISC-V System Emulator Capable of Running Linux"](https://osselcna2026.sched.com/event/2JQxM/construct-a-lean-and-fast-risc-v-system-emulator-capable-of-running-linux-jim-huang-national-cheng-kung-university?iframe=no), presenting recent progress on [rv32emu](https://github.com/sysprog21/rv32emu), including planned 64-bit support and LLVM ahead-of-time compilation.

![7f7cccc4-ac13-424f-8b5c-f74327b94cc3](att.UMu1QiHyXfzWih590DfPy050SADFLpldyEpZz4vymow.jpg)

One of my favorite talks was Rob Landley's ["Building the Simplest Possible Linux System"](https://osselcna2026.sched.com/event/2JQxw/building-the-simplest-possible-linux-system-rob-landley-hobbyist?iframe=no). He presented with no slides in a very geek style, using only terminal demos and code walkthroughs.

![13853ca3-77df-485c-9dd2-d4b492492a0b](att.re-EMcvwR3zeDv0EYGdaJ-a4QBk1ufRQhyOTqlebP7I.jpg)

The [keynote conversation between Linus Torvalds and Dirk Hohndel](https://osselcna2026.sched.com/event/2KfGq/keynote-linus-torvalds-creator-of-linux-git-in-conversation-with-dirk-hohndel-founder-dh-consulting?iframe=no) was another highlight.
